How many days a voucher is allowed before it's considered overdue.

Accurate schedule simulation walks each beat's actual visit calendar (the specific weekday for weekly and biweekly beats, weekday + week-of-month for monthly beats) to count how many visits will really happen before the credit period runs out. More precise, especially for beats with a specific collection day or an old last-visit date.
Simple fixed-interval approximation treats a visit as happening every 7 / 14 / 30 days (weekly / biweekly / monthly) and divides the days left before the credit period expires by that interval. Simpler to reason about, but can be off by several days for beats with a specific visit day or irregular history.

Every open voucher is checked against how many scheduled beat visits remain before its credit period runs out. This setting decides how to color the one visit that is the last realistic chance to collect in time — and the rest of the coloring shifts to match:
Yellow (default): 2 or more visits remaining is Green, and both "exactly 1 visit remaining" (the last chance) and "no visit scheduled before the deadline at all" are shown as Yellow — a warning, not yet urgent. The voucher only turns Red once the credit period has actually run out with no payment.
Red: 3 or more visits remaining is Green, exactly 2 is Yellow (one visit's warning before the last chance), and both "exactly 1 visit remaining" and "none scheduled" turn Red right away — don't wait for the deadline to actually pass.
